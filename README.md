**Atmospheric Scientist | Tropical Climate Dynamics | Hydroclimatology**

Personal academic website of Alejandro Jaramillo Moreno, Research Scientist at the Instituto de Ciencias de la Atmósfera y Cambio Climático (ICAyCC), Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM).

**Website:** [ajaramillomoreno.github.io](https://ajaramillomoreno.github.io)

## About

This website presents my scientific research, publications, open-source software, and ongoing projects.

My research focuses on tropical atmospheric dynamics, convection, precipitation, and climate variability, with particular interest in Mexico, Mesoamerica, the Caribbean, and tropical South America.
